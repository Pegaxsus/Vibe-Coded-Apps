Factorio: Silhouette to blueprint

This app runs completely offline, so it does not require internet access or share any information externally.

Upload a PNG with a white background and a black silhouette. The app traces the silhouette shape, reduces it to a grid, and generates a Factorio blueprint with space platform foundation tiles.
